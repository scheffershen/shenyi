Course roadmap

One practical path from prototype to confidence.

Each phase produces a concrete artifact and ties the next engineering decision to observed evidence.

Foundations & measurement

Understand the question-to-answer pipeline, trace real code, construct ground truth, and establish an MVP baseline.

Retrieval engineering

Compare semantic, keyword, and hybrid retrieval; work with chunking, metadata, filters, query handling, and reranking.

Grounded answer generation

Design context budgets, evidence-only prompts, stable citations, refusal behavior, and hallucination controls.

Reliability & product readiness

Plan graceful degradation, latency, cost, observability, security, deployment boundaries, and pilot readiness.
