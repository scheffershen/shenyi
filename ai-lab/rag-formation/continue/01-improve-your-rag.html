RAG Formation Continue · 01 · Production improvement loop

Improve your RAG from production evidence.

Start with reproducible logs and feedback, then let measured failures decide what to fix.

Supplementary field lesson

Improve one measured failure at a time.

This continuation complements the completed 16-lesson RAG Formation curriculum with a production operating loop.

Today’s tangible win

A repeatable loop: log the interaction, capture feedback, classify the failed layer, make one scoped change, and verify it on a fresh batch.

1 · Start with reproducible logs and feedback

Retain the query, request parameters, retrieved documents, final context, answer, and duration. Attach thumbs-up or thumbs-down feedback and an optional comment to the same interaction.

Without a trace, “it did not work earlier” cannot become a reproducible engineering problem.

2 · Use four complementary retrieval channels

ChannelBest atTypical question
Chunk-vectorMeaning in small passagesWhat should I do when a delivery is damaged?
Summary-vectorWhole-document scopeWhich procedure covers supplier audits?
KeywordExact identifiersWhat does SOP-2024-003 say?
GraphRelationships across sourcesWho approved this and what else do they sign off on?

3 · Triage evidence before tuning

Read real negative-feedback cases before choosing a metric. Classify each as a retrieval miss, ranking miss, unfaithful answer, over-refusal, under-refusal, or lost follow-up context.

4 · Scope the fix and verify it

Give a coding agent real examples, one failure class, one responsible layer, and a required regression test. Do not compensate for a retrieval miss by changing the generation prompt.

Six interactions are retrieval_miss cases. The document is missing from chunk-vector but present in keyword results. Check indexing, embedding, and top-k; fix that layer only. Add a regression test and rerun triage on fresh logs.

5 · Build the reliable baseline in order

  1. Parse documents into stable, referenceable text.
  2. Ship chunk-vector retrieval through one cited answer.
  3. Add summary-vector, keyword, and graph channels.
  4. Merge, deduplicate, and budget context independently of generation.
  5. Require grounded answers, citations, and honest refusal.

Retrieval practice

Question: Before changing retrieval after one bad answer, what must you capture?

Next action

Pull 20–30 negative-feedback interactions, categorize them, and choose the smallest evidence-backed change. RAG Formation Continue builds on RAG Formation; it does not change its completed 16-lesson course path.
