The guided RAG Formation course remains in development; this reference is available for self-directed practice.

Why this exists

Terminology drift compounds: once a lesson translates "grounding" one way, every later lesson that reuses the concept either repeats the mistake or introduces a second, inconsistent translation. This page is the single source every lesson, cheatsheet, and animation must match. It is a living document — extend it before writing new Chinese content that introduces a term not yet listed here, rather than inventing wording ad hoc.

術語一旦在某一課翻譯定調，後面的課要嘛延用同一個（可能有問題的）譯法，要嘛另創一個不一致的版本，問題會越滾越大。這份表格是所有課程內容、速查表、動畫都必須對齊的唯一依據。它會持續擴充——遇到表格裡還沒收錄的新詞，應該先補進來，再寫進課程內容，而不是臨時自己翻。

Locked terms

鎖定詞彙

English中文Definition
Chunking 分块 Splitting a source document into retrievable windows, with overlap to avoid cutting a fact away from its identifying context (see Lesson 6).
Reranking 重排序 Re-sorting a retriever's coarse candidate list using harder signals — exact-ID hits, metadata, cross-encoder scores (see Lesson 8).
Hallucination 幻觉 The system answers a question the corpus does not support, instead of refusing or saying it doesn't know (see Lesson 12).
Grounding / grounded 有依据 An answer is grounded when every claim in it is backed by retrieved evidence the model actually saw (see Lesson 1).
Refusal 拒答 The system declining to answer because the corpus lacks sufficient evidence, rather than guessing (see Lesson 3, Lesson 12).
