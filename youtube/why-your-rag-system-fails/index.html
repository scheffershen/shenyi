Why Your RAG System Fails (and How to Actually Fix It)

Also in: Français · 中文

Watch on YouTube ↗All videos

A short, practical walkthrough of what actually makes a Retrieval-Augmented
Generation (RAG) system work once it's in production, not just in a demo.
Five lessons drawn from real production experience.

What you'll learn:

  • Why logging every interaction and collecting thumbs-up / thumbs-down

feedback is the single highest-leverage change you can make, before
touching retrieval at all.

  • Why a bigger context window doesn't replace retrieval, and what four

things it still doesn't solve: cost and latency, access control,
freshness, and traceability.

  • Why one retrieval channel is never enough, and how chunk-vector,

summary-vector, full-text, and graph search each cover a different
blind spot.

  • How to read your own failures, categorize them with an LLM, and hand a

scoped, checkable fix to a coding agent like Claude Code or Codex,
rather than asking it to vaguely "make the RAG better."

  • A prompt you can use to build this whole four-channel pipeline

yourself, from zero.
