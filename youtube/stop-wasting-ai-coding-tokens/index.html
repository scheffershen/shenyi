Stop Wasting AI Coding Tokens: Choose the Right Tool

Also in: Français · 中文

Your AI coding agent burns through context fast — but "token waste" isn't one problem, it's five. This video breaks down RTK, Serena, codebase-memory-mcp, Context Mode, Headroom, Repomix, Code2Prompt, Gitingest, chop, and squeez, and shows you how to pick the smallest stack that actually fixes your specific bottleneck.
