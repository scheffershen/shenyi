Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026. This policy covers shenyi.dev only.

1. Overview

This is a single-page freelance portfolio site. It does not run advertising, does not sell data, and does not build advertising profiles. The only tracking on this site is Google Analytics 4 (GA4), used solely to understand traffic — which pages get visited and roughly how.

2. Cookies & consent

On your first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Until you click "Accept," Google Analytics runs in a consent-denied state and does not store any identifying cookie. If you click "Decline," it stays denied. Your choice is remembered in your browser so you aren't asked again on later visits.

3. What Google Analytics collects (only if you accept)

  • Pages viewed and time spent on them
  • Referrer / traffic source (e.g. search engine, social link)
  • Device, browser and operating system type
  • Approximate location (country/city, derived from IP — GA4 does not log your full IP address)
  • General engagement metrics (sessions, bounce rate, scroll depth)

No name, email address, or contact-form content is ever sent to Google Analytics.

4. Data retention

Event-level Google Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months (GA4's standard default setting), after which it is automatically deleted.

5. The contact form is separate

The contact form on the homepage submits directly to Formspree, a third-party form processor, so your message can reach me by email. This is unrelated to Google Analytics and is not affected by your cookie choice.

6. Withdrawing consent

You can change your mind at any time using "Cookie preferences" in the footer of any page — it reopens the banner so you can accept or decline again. You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser, or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

7. Google's own policy

Google Analytics is operated by Google LLC. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google itself processes data.
