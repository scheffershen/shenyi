Hybrid-Retrieval RAG System for Pharmaceutical Quality Management

A hybrid-retrieval RAG assistant that indexes internal quality-management documentation (SOPs, risk analyses, audit and supplier-qualification questionnaires) for a pharmaceutical group. It also generates a knowledge-graph wiki and supports questionnaire extraction and answers with AI.

Indexed 884 documents (868 succeeded) in a dense + keyword + knowledge-graph retrieval pipeline, which automatically extracted 6,673 entities across 425 wiki pages.
